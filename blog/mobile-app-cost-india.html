How Much Does a Mobile App Cost in India in 2026?

A realistic budget guide for startups and businesses planning their first mobile app.

Home / Blog / Mobile App Cost in India
Back to Blog
App Development • Published September 17, 2026 • CyberNovaWorks Team

"How much does a mobile app cost in India?" A simple utility app and a full marketplace are both "apps", but their budgets are worlds apart. This guide gives you honest Indian-market ranges so you can budget before talking to developers and avoid surprises.

Mobile App Cost Ranges in India (2026)

  • Simple single-purpose app: ₹80,000 – ₹2,00,000. A calculator, timer or utility app with one core function and a clean interface.
  • Startup MVP (Minimum Viable Product): ₹80,000 – ₹2,50,000. Login, core feature, basic UI — built to test your idea with real users fast.
  • Full-featured app: ₹2,00,000 – ₹6,00,000. Multiple features, chat, payments, profiles, push notifications and an admin panel.
  • Complex platforms: ₹6,00,000+. Marketplaces, on-demand services, social networks or apps with heavy real-time functionality.

The Biggest Cost Drivers

  • Number of platforms: Android only, iOS only, or both. Each platform adds development and testing time.
  • Backend and server: Any app that stores user data, syncs or processes payments needs a server — a major cost many first-timers forget.
  • Admin panel: If you need to manage users, content or orders from a dashboard, that's a whole second product.
  • Complex features: Real-time chat, maps, AI, video calls and payment gateways all increase both time and price.

Why Building an MVP First Is Smarter

Most successful apps started as a small MVP to test demand. You get a working app in weeks instead of months, learn what users actually want, and only then invest in the full product. Starting with the full feature list usually means paying for features nobody uses.

App Store Costs You Shouldn't Forget

Beyond development, budget for the Google Play registration (US$25 one-time) and Apple Developer Program (US$99/year). Ongoing server costs and app maintenance (updates, fixes) typically run 15–20% of the build cost per year.
