How Much Does a Logo Cost in India in 2026?

A practical price guide for your business logo — whether you're a new startup or rebranding.

Home / Blog / Logo Design Cost in India
Back to Blog
Graphic Design • Published September 18, 2026 • CyberNovaWorks Team

"How much does a logo cost in India?" is one of the most common questions small businesses ask us. The honest answer: anywhere from ₹0 to ₹50,000+. The right price depends on what you actually need — a quick mark for a side project, or a brand identity you'll use for the next decade. Here's the complete breakdown.

Logo Price Ranges in India (2026)

  • Free logo generators: ₹0. AI tools like Canva or Looka-style generators give you a printable mark in minutes, but the design isn't unique to you — dozens of businesses can get the same look.
  • Freelancers and gig platforms: ₹1,000 – ₹8,000. Budget-friendly, but quality and file formats vary widely. Always ask for source files before paying.
  • Professional agencies (like an entry package): ₹5,000 – ₹25,000. Includes 2–3 concepts, unlimited revisions until approval, and delivery of print-ready files.
  • Full brand identity: ₹25,000 – ₹50,000+. Logo plus colour palette, typography, stationery and brand guidelines.

Why Cheap Logos End Up Costing More

A ₹500 logo from an unverified source often arrives as a low-resolution JPEG — useless for print, banners or packaging. Rebuying a proper logo later costs more than doing it right the first time. What matters is that you always own the source files (AI, SVG, EPS) in every colour variant, so your brand never gets stuck.

What Should a Professional Logo Package Include?

  • 2–3 initial concepts based on your business and audience
  • Unlimited revision rounds until you're satisfied
  • Print and web versions (RGB + CMYK) and PNG/SVG/PDF files
  • Full source files — so you own the design forever

Logo + Branding Bundles Save Money

Getting your logo designed together with your brochures, business cards and social media kits in one project costs far less than hiring different people separately. A consistent brand looks more professional and builds trust faster with customers.
