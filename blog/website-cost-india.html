How Much Does a Website Cost in India in 2026?

An honest, transparent price guide for Indian businesses planning a website this year.

Home / Blog / Website Cost in India
Back to Blog
Web Development • Published September 14, 2026 • CyberNovaWorks Team

"How much does a website cost in India?" is the first question almost every client asks us — and the answer is rarely straightforward because a "website" can mean anything from a five-page business site to a full e-commerce platform. This guide gives you realistic ballpark figures so you can plan your budget before you talk to any development company.

Typical Website Costs in India in 2026

Based on our project experience and the current Indian market, here are the price bands you can expect:

  • Basic business website (5–10 pages): ₹15,000 – ₹40,000. Includes design, content upload, contact form, and mobile responsiveness.
  • Business website with custom design and CMS: ₹40,000 – ₹80,000. More customisation, easier editing, additional pages and animations.
  • E-commerce store (50–200 products): ₹40,000 – ₹1,50,000. Product catalogues, payment gateway (UPI, cards), shipping and admin panel.
  • Custom web application / portal: ₹1,50,000 – ₹5,00,000+. Dashboards, booking systems, CRMs or SaaS platforms rarely cost less.

What Makes a Quote Go Up or Down?

Be wary of quotes that seem too good (or too scary) to be true. Ask what's included. The biggest price drivers are:

  • Number of pages and content: More pages and more custom layouts mean more design and coding work.
  • E-commerce features: Payment gateways, GST-compliant invoices, logistics and inventory integrations add real cost.
  • Custom functionality: Booking engines, member logins, third-party API integrations and automation all add to the price.
  • Ongoing costs: Domain (₹600–₹1,000/year), hosting (₹3,000–₹15,000/year) and maintenance plans (₹500–₹5,000/month).

Watch Out for Hidden Gaps

A cheap quote often excludes mobile design, SEO basics, SSL, security or content upload — leaving you to pay for them later. Always ask for a written scope that lists exactly what's included before signing anything. Our recommendation: choose a web development partner who gives a fixed-price quote and a milestone-by-milestone delivery plan.

Is a Cheaper Website Always Better?

Not necessarily. A ₹5,000 template site might cost you more in the long run through lost customers. On the other hand, a ₹3 lakh enterprise site is overkill for a local clinic. Spend what matches your revenue goals. Most Indian businesses are well served by the ₹25,000 – ₹60,000 range for a solid, mobile-first business website.
