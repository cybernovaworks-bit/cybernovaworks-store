Why Swapfile is different

Most online file converters upload your document to a server before processing it. Swapfile does the work directly in your browser using JavaScript, so your files never leave your device — no privacy trade-off, no waiting on an upload.

Common questions

Is Swapfile free?
Yes — every tool is free to use, with no account or sign-up.
Are my files uploaded anywhere?
No. Conversion happens locally in your browser using JavaScript; files never leave your device.
Is there a file size limit?
Only the limits of your own device's memory — very large files may be slower to process.
