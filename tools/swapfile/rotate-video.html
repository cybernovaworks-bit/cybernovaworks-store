    Common questions

    Why do the dimensions change?
    A 90° turn swaps width and height, so a 1920×1080 clip becomes 1080×1920. That is what makes a sideways clip fill a phone screen properly.
    Can I mirror a selfie video?
    Yes — pick Flip horizontal. Front cameras normally mirror what you see, so this puts it back the way other people see you.
    Does rotating lose quality?
    It re-encodes at a high quality setting, so there is no visible loss.
